QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Some answers you may need before you write to us.

Do I need experience to join a trip?

No. Many of our treks and camps are designed for beginners. Read the difficulty guide on our Types & Styles page, then tell us your experience in the form above.

What is the best season to climb in Pakistan?

The main climbing season in the Karakoram is from June to August. Treks to lower valleys are often possible from May to October.

Do you provide equipment?

Group equipment such as tents, ropes and stoves is provided on guided trips. You will need your own boots and clothing layers; see the gear checklist on our Types & Styles page.

How do I book a shelter?

Open the Sheltering page, click any shelter and fill in the short booking form.
